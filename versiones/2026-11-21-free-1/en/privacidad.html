Privacy policy

AFTER 0.5.0 is free, works offline and contains no ads or purchases. All available games and categories are unlocked.

1. Controller and scope

Controller: Fabián Del Castillo, Ecuador. Privacy and support contact: fabijuegos10@gmail.com.

This policy covers AFTER 0.5.0 for Android. The app works offline and does not send data to AFTER servers, advertising services or analytics tools. No account is required.

2. Data stored on the device

Names or nicknames are optional and identify participants. They are stored locally until you remove them from Players or clear AFTER’s data.

The language and preferences for sound, vibration, reduced motion and onboarding are saved to remember your settings.

When you accept the terms, the latest acceptance is stored: document version and hashes, UTC date according to the device, app version, language and confirmation that you are at least 16. The record does not store your birth date, identity or player names.

If you update from an earlier test, simulated package preferences and ad choices may remain. This version does not use them to enable advertising or charges. You can remove them by clearing the app’s data.

3. During games

Charades processes the phone’s tilt. Finger Picker uses touch positions; it does not read fingerprints or identify people.

Roles, votes, words and game positions stay in memory, without a remote history. Random selections are for entertainment.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access. This Android edition has no Internet permission and does not activate ad or purchase services.

4. Retention and deletion

You can delete names in Players and acceptance in Settings > Privacy > Delete local record. To delete all data, use Android Settings > Apps > AFTER > Storage > Clear data.

Local data stays until you delete or replace it. The app uses the system’s private storage and disables its Android backup. Manufacturer transfer tools may follow different rules.

5. Support and website

If you email support, we receive your address, message and attachments to handle your request, meet applicable obligations and resolve issues. Do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents.

Requests are retained while being handled and, generally, for up to 90 days after resolution. A legal obligation or claim may require specific information to be kept longer.

Email and the document website on GitHub Pages are external services. Their providers may process technical data, such as a website visitor’s IP address, outside Ecuador. This does not give them the names, roles or games stored in AFTER.

Provider policies: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement and https://policies.google.com/privacy

You will be informed before significant changes in data use. Reading this policy or accepting the terms does not consent to advertising or new optional uses.
